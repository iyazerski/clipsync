Built to stay out of your way

Universal Clipboard for people who mix Mac, Windows, and Android.

Mac, Windows & Android

Copy a link on your phone and paste it on your work PC. Copy an address on your Mac and paste it into Maps on your phone.

Works over any network

Home Wi-Fi, office network, or mobile data. Your devices don't need to be on the same LAN or sitting next to each other.

End-to-end encrypted

Every copy is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM before it leaves. The relay can't read your clips or your device names.

Skips your passwords

Automatically ignores sensitive copies marked private by password managers on macOS and Windows.

No account needed

Pair computers with a one-time short code and phones by scanning a QR code. No sign-up, no email, no tracking.

Public relay or self-host

Works right away on the free public relay, or run your own tiny Go relay in Docker in under a minute.

Prefer to host your own relay?

The public relay at clip.iyazerski.dev only sees encrypted data, but you can run your own private relay on any Docker host (x86-64 or ARM64).

In clipsync, choose Use your own relay, copy your Relay token from Settings, and run Docker Compose. Caddy handles HTTPS automatically.

Read the self-hosting guide →
Docker Compose + Caddy 
curl -fsSLO https://raw.githubusercontent.com/iyazerski/clipsync/main/relay/compose.yaml
printf 'RELAY_DOMAIN=clip.example.com\nCLIPSYNC_TOKEN=<your token>\n' > .env
docker compose up -d

Common questions

Is my clipboard really private?

Yes. Your devices share a private key and encrypt every copy with AES-256-GCM before sending it. The relay only passes encrypted bytes between your devices, keeps the latest clip in memory for 2 minutes, and can't read your text or device names.

Why do Mac or Windows show a warning the first time?

The desktop apps aren't code-signed with Apple or Microsoft yet. Using the Terminal or PowerShell install command above skips the warning. If you download the installer directly, on Mac open the app then click System Settings → Privacy & Security → Open Anyway, and on Windows click More info → Run anyway.

Why does sending from Android take a tap?

Android blocks apps from reading the clipboard in the background. Receiving copies on Android is completely automatic, and sending from your phone takes one tap: highlight text and pick Send to computer, share text to clipsync, or tap the Send clipboard Quick Settings tile.

Does it sync images or files?

No. clipsync syncs plain text only, up to 256 KB. There is no clipboard history, and the latest copy wins.

Does it work on iPhone or Linux?

Not yet. clipsync currently supports macOS 13+, Windows, and Android 14+.

How do I remove a lost or old device?

Open Devices on a computer, click ⋯ next to the device, and pick Remove…. That creates a fresh pairing key so the removed device can't sync anymore, and you re-add your other devices with a new code.
